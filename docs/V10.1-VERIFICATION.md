## 7. Known limitations

- Relevance phrase vocabularies (conflict query, explicit audit,
  outcome-marker normalisation) are English-only; canonical-object triggers
  are language-independent.
- Prediction↔outcome and unknown↔evidence matching require both sides to
  project to a canonical proposition with matching subject+predicate; a
  non-proposition-shaped outcome yields no link (honest UNKNOWN rather than
  a guessed match).
- The bounded re-audit runs when a confirmation arrives through the public
  confirm endpoint; direct internal `MaintenanceProposalService.confirm()`
  calls (tests, tools) do not implicitly re-audit — callers opt in via
  `maintenance_runtime.reaudit_after_confirmation`.
- Real-model behavior of the whole V10.1 loop is unverified in this
  environment (NOT CONNECTED).
